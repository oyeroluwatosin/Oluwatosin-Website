How I work

Five steps, and what I do differently at each.

  1. UnderstandRead the spec or PRD, then check it for gaps before any screen is drawn. At DSN an AI-assisted check found user-need gaps in 9 of 10 recent projects.See it in DSN →
  2. DefineWrite down each role and what it may do. At TACEF that meant every admin sees only what they are allowed to manage.See it in TACEF →
  3. DesignFlows and Figma prototypes with error and status states drawn in from the start, not added at the end.See it in the Friction Lab →
  4. TestPut it in front of real users. KloFood testing showed unclear wording and weak reasons to come back, so I now test earlier.See it in KloFood →
  5. Hand offSpecs and components developers can build from. When engineers flagged flows missing from the PRD, I worked through them together.See it in DSN →

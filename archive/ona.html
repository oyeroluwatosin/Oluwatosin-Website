About — the designer behind ỌNÀ

Hi, I’m Oluwatosin — Tosin to most. A UX designer who thrives at the intersection of data and design.

For 3+ years I’ve specialised in finding and fixing the friction points that hold users back — turning wordy interfaces into intuitive flows and low-converting websites into experiences that perform. I’ve led teams of up to eight, run research from pilot interviews to thematic coding, and shipped enterprise dashboards with zero-trust security models.

Clarity over clutter. Always.

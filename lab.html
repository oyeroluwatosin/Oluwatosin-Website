Friction Lab · interactive

Don't read about how I work. Try it.

My strength is finding the friction that gets in a user's way and designing it back out. This lab lets you do the same: spot the problems in a broken screen, change the phone's conditions and watch the states hold up, then see one screen through four user roles.

Concept screens built in HTML. Not client work, and not a shipped product.
Mode 01 · heuristic evaluation

This scanner app has five problems. Tap the phone to find them.

A warehouse worker opens ScanFlow mid-shift. The Wi-Fi has dropped and nothing explains what's going on. Each find shows the usability heuristic it breaks, how severe it is on Nielsen's 0–4 scale, and what I'd change.

    Mode 03 · role-based design

    Same review batch, four people. Each sees only what their role allows.

    Based on a pattern from my work as sole designer on a QA review platform at Data Science Nigeria: four roles, an admin layer for critical actions, and a status that updates itself when every task is done. This is an illustrative recreation, not the real interface.
